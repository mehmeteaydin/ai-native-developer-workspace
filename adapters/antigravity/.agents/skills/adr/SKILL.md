# /adr

Use `prompts/adr.md` for the decision question.
First discuss options with a recommendation and revisit triggers (use `ask_question` with `(Recommended)` on your proposed choice); wait for the human decision; only then write `docs/decisions/NNNN-<slug>.md` from the template, with honest consequences (benefits AND costs).
